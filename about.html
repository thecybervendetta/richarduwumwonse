Career aspirations

I want to work on a red team or VAPT team, testing real systems and writing the remediation advice that gets them fixed. Along the way I want to keep building tools that make testing safer and repeatable, the way my recon engine refuses to touch anything out of scope.

I believe security works best when offense directly informs defensive engineering, closing loops rather than just finding flaws.
